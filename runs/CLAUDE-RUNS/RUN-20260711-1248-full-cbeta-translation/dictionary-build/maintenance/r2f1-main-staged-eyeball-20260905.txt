=== t_351fa42d396b 粥飯 staged s1 Explanation ===
     The joined food words also name a monastery's scheduled communal meals and their provision.
     The Imperially Revised Baizhang Monastic Code, redacted by Dongyang Dehui, schedules gruel and rice at two times and tea and hot water at three (二時上粥飯。三時上茶湯。). [CJK]
     Zhaozhou Congshen likewise says that, during his travels, the two scheduled gruel-and-rice meals were the only occasions that divided his attention (除二時粥飯是雜用心處). [CJK]
     Baoning Renyong calls himself a gruel-and-rice host (粥飯主人) who receives travellers from north and south with salt, vinegar, sufficient gruel, and sufficient rice. [CJK]
     Here the headword is not merely a list of two foods: it names recurring communal provision, its timetable, and the host responsible for it.
     Zen bends the ordinary food pair into the institutional vocabulary of monastery scheduling and hospitality.
